## Qué vas a aprender

- Contar un día típico: *get up*, *have a shower*, *go to work*, *go to bed*.
- Colocar adverbios de frecuencia: *always, usually, sometimes, never*.
- Preguntar horarios: *What time do you usually get up?*
- Decir si eres *a morning person*.

## Explicación

Los adverbios de frecuencia dicen *con qué frecuencia*:

| 100% | | 50% | | 0% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| always | usually | sometimes | | never |

Van **delante** del verbo principal: *I always have breakfast.* No *Always I have breakfast* ni *I have always breakfast.*

Van **después** de *be*: *I'm always tired in the morning.*

Con *never* el verbo va en afirmativo: *He never eats meat.* No *He doesn't never eat meat.*

En preguntas, el adverbio va después del sujeto: *Do you usually go shopping on Saturday?* No *Usually do you go…?*

Un día típico se cuenta en orden: mañana (*get up, have breakfast, have a shower, go to work*), tarde (*have lunch, finish work, go home, go shopping, go to the gym*), noche (*make dinner, have dinner, do housework, watch TV, have a bath, go to bed*).

*make* dinner / coffee, pero *do* housework / homework. *go to work, go to school, go to bed, go home* (no *go to home*). *go to the gym, go to the cinema*.

Hannah trabaja en la BBC. Su hijo Kit tiene tres años. Las ocho preguntas de *Are you a morning person?* sirven para ti: hora de levantarte, si estás cansado/a, ducha o baño, desayuno, hora de salir, si tienes prisa, si te gustan las mañanas.

USA *an apartment* = UK *a flat*. USA *the subway* = UK *the Underground*.

## Vocabulario

**Por la mañana**

- **get up** — levantarse
- **have breakfast** — desayunar
- **have a shower** — ducharse
- **go to work (by bus, train, car)** — ir al trabajo (en autobús, tren, coche)
- **have a coffee** — tomar un café

**Por la tarde**

- **have lunch** — comer
- **finish work** — terminar de trabajar
- **go home** — irse a casa
- **go shopping** — ir de compras
- **go to the gym** — ir al gimnasio

**Por la noche**

- **make dinner** — preparar la cena
- **have dinner** — cenar
- **do housework** — hacer las tareas de casa
- **watch TV** — ver la tele
- **have a bath** — bañarse
- **go to bed** — acostarse

**Frecuencia y tiempo**

- **always / usually / sometimes / never** — siempre / normalmente / a veces / nunca
- **a morning person** — una persona mañanera
- **then / after breakfast** — entonces / después del desayuno
- **at the weekend** — el fin de semana
- **in the morning / afternoon / evening** — por la mañana / tarde / noche

Sonidos (di en voz alta):

- **/j/** (*yacht*): yes, you, young, yellow
- **/juː/**: usually, student, music, beautiful

## Frases modelo

- What time do you usually get up? I get up at 7.00.
- I always get up at 8.00, because I start work at 9.00.
- I never drink coffee, because I don't like it.
- I usually finish work at 6.00, but on Fridays I finish at 3.00.
- I sometimes watch TV, or I read and listen to music.
- I never make dinner. My father makes it. We usually have dinner at half past eight.

¿Eres persona mañanera? (responde tú):

- What time do you usually get up?
- Do you usually feel tired?
- Do you have a shower or a bath in the morning?
- Do you always have breakfast? Where?
- What do you have for breakfast?
- What time do you go to work (school / university)?
- Do you usually need to hurry in the morning?
- Do you like mornings? Why (not)?

Ritmo (di en voz alta):

- What time do you usually have lunch? At half past one.
- What time do you usually have dinner? At about eight o'clock.
- What time do you usually go to bed? At half past eleven.
