## Qué vas a aprender

- Preguntar con *Who, What, Where, How, How old*.
- Decir números de teléfono y los números del 11 al 100.
- Distinguir *thirteen* / *thirty*, *fourteen* / *forty*, etc.
- Rellenar una ficha: nombre, nacionalidad, edad, teléfono, correo.

## Explicación

Las preguntas *Wh-* y *How* con *be* siguen este orden: palabra interrogativa + verbo + sujeto.

| Palabra | Verbo | Sujeto | Respuesta |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who | 's | Tom? | He's a friend. |
| What | 's | your email? | johng@gmail.com. |
| Where | are | you from? | I'm from Brighton in England. |
| When | 's | the concert? | It's on Tuesday. |
| How | are | you? | I'm fine, thanks. |
| How old | is | she? | She's ten. |

Puedes contraer *is* detrás de la palabra interrogativa: *What's her name? = What is her name?* *Where's he from? = Where is he from?* *How's Anna? How old's Jan?*

No contraigas si la última palabra es un pronombre: *How old is she?* (no *How old's she?*). *Where is he?* (no *Where's he?*).

En teléfonos, el 0 se dice *oh* (o *zero*). Se agrupan los dígitos. En correos: *@* = *at*, *.* = *dot*.

Cuidado con el acento: *thirTEEN* frente a *THIRty*; *fourTEEN* frente a *FORty*.

Números 11–20: *eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen, twenty*. Luego *twenty-one*, *thirty*, *forty* (sin *u*), *fifty*, *sixty*, *seventy*, *eighty*, *ninety*, *a hundred*.

## Vocabulario

- **Who…?** — ¿quién?
- **What…?** — ¿qué?
- **Where…?** — ¿dónde?
- **When…?** — ¿cuándo?
- **How…? / How old…?** — ¿cómo? / ¿cuántos años?
- **phone number** — número de teléfono
- **email address** — correo electrónico
- **@ = at / . = dot** — arroba / punto
- **bus / bus stop** — autobús / parada
- **class** — clase
- **How are you? I'm fine, thanks.** — ¿Qué tal? Bien, gracias.
- **eleven – twenty** — once – veinte
- **twenty-one, twenty-two, thirty, thirty-three** — 21, 22, 30, 33
- **forty, fifty, sixty, seventy, eighty, ninety** — 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90
- **a hundred** — cien
- **address** — dirección

Pares que se confunden (di ambos en voz alta):

- 13 thirteen / 30 thirty
- 14 fourteen / 40 forty
- 15 fifteen / 50 fifty
- 16 sixteen / 60 sixty
- 17 seventeen / 70 seventy
- 18 eighteen / 80 eighty
- 19 nineteen / 90 ninety

## Frases modelo

- What's your phone number? It's 07710 097456.

Pia y Lin hablan de Alex:

- Pia: Who's he?
- Lin: He's Alex. He's in my class.
- Pia: Where's he from?
- Lin: He's from Mexico.
- Pia: How old is he?
- Lin: He's 22, I think.
- Pia: He's very good-looking!

En la calle:

- Lin: Hi, Pia. How are you?
- Pia: Hi, Lin. I'm fine, and you?
- Lin: I'm fine, too. This is Alex. He's in my class. Alex, this is Pia.
- Pia: Hi, Alex!
- Alex: Hi. What class are you in?
- Lin: That's my bus! Bye. See you tomorrow!
- Alex: Bye. Where are you from, Pia?
- Pia: I'm from Mexico. This is my bus stop. Bye, Alex. Nice to meet you.
- Alex: Nice to meet you, too, Pia. What's your phone number?
- Pia: Sorry, my bus! It's 07365…

Cuadro de preguntas:

- A: Where are you from? B: I'm from Germany.
- A: How are you? B: Fine, thanks.
- A: Who's Alex? B: He's a friend.
- A: What's your name? B: Molly.
- A: Where's Modena? B: It's in Italy.
- A: How old are you? B: 26.
- A: What's your phone number? B: 07702 960836.

Números impresos para practicar: *028 964 96083* · *08081 570724* · *0131 496 0638*.
