## Qué vas a aprender

- Juntar pasado de *be*, regulares e irregulares en un mismo relato.
- Irregulares nuevos: *buy, leave, say, see, send, sit, tell, write*.
- Preguntar *Where did he see her? What did they talk about?*
- Leer *Strangers on a train* y escribir el final.

## Explicación

En un relato real se mezclan tres sistemas. No elijas uno y olvides los otros.

1. **be**: *I was, she was, the train was full. Was it expensive?* No uses *did* con *be*: *Was it slow?* no *Did it was slow?*
2. **Regulares**: *opened, looked, moved, started, talked, arrived, asked, answered, smiled, invited.* Negación y pregunta: *didn't open, Did she smile?*
3. **Irregulares**: cambian la forma en la afirmación. En *didn't* y *Did* vuelve el infinitivo.

| Infinitivo | Pasado | − / ? |
|---|---|---|
| buy | bought | didn't buy / Did you buy? |
| leave | left | didn't leave / Did she leave? |
| say | said | didn't say / What did he say? |
| see | saw | didn't see / Where did you see? |
| send | sent | didn't send / Did they send? |
| sit | sat | didn't sit / Did you sit? |
| tell | told | didn't tell / What did she tell you? |
| write | wrote | didn't write / Did he write? |
| go | went | didn't go / Where did you go? |
| have | had | didn't have / Did you have? |
| get | got | didn't get / Did you get? |
| do | did | didn't do / What did you do? |

Ortografía regular del quiz:

- Dobla la consonante: *stop → stopped*.
- Solo *-d* (ya terminan en *e*): *arrive, exchange, move, phone*.
- *-ed* se dice /ɪd/ en *start* y *wait* (terminan en *t*).

Pregunta: *Did* + sujeto + infinitivo. *Where did you go on Saturday?* no *Where did you went?* Afirmación: *I went shopping. I bought a new jacket.*

David va en un tren lleno. En el andén ve a una mujer alta, rubia, de ojos azules. Ella se sienta a su lado. Huelen Chanel Nº 5. Hablan de Chopin. En Paddington ella invita a un café.

## Vocabulario

**Regulares del quiz:** *arrive, ask, exchange, finish, look, move, open, phone, start, stop, talk, wait*

**Irregulares**

- **buy → bought** — comprar
- **leave → left** — irse / dejar
- **say → said** — decir
- **see → saw** — ver
- **send → sent** — enviar
- **sit → sat** — sentarse
- **tell → told** — contar / decirle a alguien
- **write → wrote** — escribir

**El relato**

- **a platform** — un andén
- **a nice smell** — un olor agradable
- **classical music** — música clásica
- **a message** — un mensaje
- **tickets for a concert** — entradas para un concierto
- **a concert hall** — una sala de conciertos
- **a seat** — un asiento
- **full** — lleno
- **exchange** — intercambiar
- **turn on a light** — encender una luz
- **That's interesting.** — Qué interesante.
- **Time to go.** — Es hora de irse.
- **property** — inmuebles
- **a stranger** — un desconocido

## Frases modelo

*Strangers on a train* — Part 1

When the train stopped at the station, I opened my eyes and looked out of the window. I saw a woman on the platform. She was tall and blonde with blue eyes. The train moved and I closed my eyes. It was 4.00 p.m. and the train was full.

'Excuse me. Can I sit here?' I opened my eyes again. It was the tall, blonde woman.

'Sure,' I answered. She sat down next to me. There was a nice smell. Chanel No. 5. I started to listen to music on my phone.

'I love Chopin.'

'Sorry?' I said.

'You're listening to Chopin. I love classical music.' She smiled. Her eyes were very blue.

We talked about music until the train arrived at Paddington Station. 'Would you like a cup of coffee?' she asked me. I looked at my watch. 'OK,' I said. 'I have time.'

Preguntas:

1. Where did the man first see the woman?
2. What was the woman's perfume?
3. What did they talk about?
4. What did the woman invite him to do when the train arrived?

Part 2

We bought two coffees at the station café. We sat down.

'My name's Olivia,' she said.

'I'm David. I'm from Chicago. Nice to meet you. What do you do?' I asked.

'I work in property – flats and houses. What do you do?'

'I work for Citibank.'

'That's interesting!' said Olivia. 'Do you live in London?'

'Yes, but I'm new here. I moved to London last month. I have a flat in Chelsea.'

'Wow! That's an expensive part of London!'

I looked at my watch. 'Time to go. I have a tennis lesson this evening.'

'I can drive you home,' she said. 'I live near Chelsea.' I told her my address.

Preguntas:

5. What's Olivia's job?
6. What's David's job?
7. Where do they live?
8. Why did David give Olivia his address?

Gramática (elige):

1. A: Where did you **go** on Saturday? B: I **went** shopping for clothes. A: What did you **buy**? B: I **bought** a new jacket. A: **Was** it expensive? B: No, it **wasn't**.
2. A: What **did you do** last night? B: I **went** to the cinema. I **saw** a new French film. A: **Did** you **like** it? B: No, I **didn't like** it very much. It **was** very slow.

Las partes 3–5 no están impresas aquí. Con las fotos (un coche, una sala de conciertos, un edificio iluminado de noche) **escribe tú** el final:

- What kind of car did Olivia have?
- What did she have two tickets for?
- Where did she want to meet him? What time?
- What time did David arrive at the concert hall?
- What happened at 7.45? What did David do then and after that?
- Why do you think Olivia didn't come to the concert?
- What do you think David saw when he opened the door?
- Do you think it was a good ending? Why (not)?
