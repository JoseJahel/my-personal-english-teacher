## Qué vas a aprender

- Repasar *present continuous* frente a *present simple*.
- Repasar *there is / there are* y *was / were*.
- Activar ropa, viaje, hotel y *in / on / at / under*.
- Leer posts de un fin de semana en Barcelona y comprobar lo que ya puedes decir.

## Explicación

Este bloque junta Files 9 y 10. Antes de los ejercicios, recuerda cuatro contrastes:

1. **Ahora** = continuous: *Are you working at the moment? It's raining. I'm wearing a skirt today.*
   **Rutina** = simple: *Do you usually go to work by train? I usually wear trousers.*

2. *be* en continuous: *She isn't staying* (no *doesn't staying*). *He 's doing his homework now.*

3. **Hay**: *There are two beds. There aren't any windows. Are there any towels? Yes, there is / there are.*

4. **Pasado de be**: *It was very hot last summer. Where were you yesterday? She wasn't at school. Were they at home?*

*in / on / under* = posición de un objeto. *at / in / on* = tipo de lugar (*at home, in the kitchen, on a train*).

Ropa frecuente: *shoes, dress, jacket, shirt, skirt, suit, trousers, hat.*

Hotel: *Reception, lift, gift shop, bathroom, car park, restaurant.*

Viaje en continuous: *waiting, phoning, carrying, arriving, wearing, staying.*

## Vocabulario

**Viaje (continuous)**

- **staying** — alojándose
- **waiting** — esperando
- **phoning** — llamando
- **carrying** — llevando (una maleta)
- **arriving** — llegando
- **wearing** — llevando puesto

**Ropa**

- **shoes** — zapatos
- **a dress** — un vestido
- **a jacket** — una chaqueta
- **a shirt** — una camisa
- **a skirt** — una falda
- **a suit** — un traje
- **trousers** — pantalones
- **a hat** — un sombrero

**Hotel**

- **Reception** — recepción
- **a lift** — un ascensor
- **a gift shop** — una tienda de regalos
- **the bathroom** — el baño
- **a car park** — un aparcamiento

**Lugar**

- **in / on / under** — dentro / sobre / debajo
- **at home / in the kitchen / on a train** — en casa / en la cocina / en un tren

Palabras 9 y 10 para decir en voz alta: *a living room, the box office, traffic, darling, a private investigator, a client, a tourist destination, a great view, Enjoy your stay, a robbery, a suspect, last night.*

Acento (subraya la sílaba fuerte y di en voz alta): *sweater, travel, arrive, restaurant, cupboard, Reception.*

## Frases modelo

Gramática (elige *a* o *b*):

0. ____'s your name? a Who **b What**
1. Are you ____ at the moment? a work **b working**
2. She ____ staying in a very good hotel. **a isn't** b doesn't
3. He ____ his homework now. a doing **b 's doing**
4. A ____ raining? B No, it isn't. **a Is it** b It's
5. Can you talk now, or ____? **a are you driving** b do you drive
6. ____ to work by train? **a Do you usually go** b Are you usually going
7. I usually wear trousers, but today ____ a skirt. a I wear **b I'm wearing**
8. ____ two beds in the room. a There is **b There are**
9. There aren't ____ windows. a some **b any**
10. ____ any towels in the bathroom? **a Are there** b There are
11. Is there a swimming pool? Yes, ____. a there's **b there is**
12. It ____ very hot last summer. **a was** b were
13. Where ____ you yesterday at six o'clock? a was **b were**
14. Karen ____ at school yesterday. **a wasn't** b not was
15. ____ at home last night? a They were **b Were they**
