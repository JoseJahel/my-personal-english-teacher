## Qué vas a aprender

- Repasar *was/were*, regulares e irregulares en la misma frase.
- Elegir *be, have* o *do* en expresiones (*have a good day, be tired, do sport*).
- Pronunciar *-ed* (/t/, /d/, /ɪd/) y vocales distintas (*said* ≠ *rained*).
- Contar el sábado pasado, las vacaciones y *the last time you…*

## Explicación

Esta lección no añade un tiempo nuevo. Junta lo de 10B, 11A, 11B y 12A en preguntas que puedes **escribir** o **decir en voz alta**.

Tres motores:

- *be* → *was / were / wasn't / weren't*. Pregunta sin *did*: *Where were you yesterday at 7.00?*
- Regular → *liked, hated, wanted, needed, opened, watched, decided.* *didn't like*, *Did you like?*
- Irregular → *got, went, had, did, bought, saw, said, left, sent, sat, told, wrote.* *didn't go*, *Did you go?*

Collocations que no se improvisan:

| *be* | *have* | *do* | *get* | *go* | *wait* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| be tired | have a good day | do sport | get up | go shopping | wait for a bus |
| be hungry | have breakfast | do homework | get a bus / a train | go to the country | |
| | have lunch | do housework | | | |
| | have a nice evening | | | | |

*-ed* /ɪd/ solo si el verbo termina en *t* o *d*: *hated, wanted, decided, needed.* *liked, loved, opened, watched* no son /ɪd/.

Vocales que fallan:

- *said* /sed/ no rima con *rained / painted / waited* /eɪ/.
- *liked* /laɪkt/ no rima con *did, listened, lived*.
- *worked* /wɜːkt/ no rima con *bought, saw, talked* /ɔː/.

*last Saturday, last summer, last night, yesterday morning, last week, last birthday* anclan el pasado. *the last time you…* pide una anécdota: *The last film I saw was… I saw it in… I liked / didn't like it.*

Las casillas verdes y rosas del tablero original eran de grupo. Aquí las haces tú: escribe **dos frases distintas**, no copies la primera.

## Vocabulario

- **What did you do last Saturday?** — ¿Qué hiciste el sábado pasado?
- **I went to the country.** — Fui al campo.
- **a souvenir** — un recuerdo (de viaje)
- **a stranger** — un desconocido
- **a programme** — un programa (de tele)
- **exercise** — ejercicio
- **a message / an email** — un mensaje / un correo
- **use a laptop** — usar un portátil
- **say sorry** — pedir perdón
- **have a good day** — pasar un buen día
- **be tired / hungry** — estar cansado / tener hambre
- **do sport / homework / housework** — hacer deporte / deberes / tareas

Irregulares de esta unidad: *be – was/were, buy – bought, do – did, get – got, go – went, have – had, leave – left, say – said, see – saw, send – sent, sit – sat, tell – told, write – wrote.*

Regulares: *ask, arrive, book, change, cook, decide, finish, hate, like, listen, live, love, need, open, pack, paint, park, play, rain, relax, stay, study, talk, travel, wait, want, watch, work.*

## Frases modelo

Modelo de respuesta larga:

- Last Saturday I got up at 9.00. I had breakfast at home. Then I went to the city centre and I bought a souvenir. I didn't do any sport. In the evening I saw a film. I liked it.
- On my last summer holiday I went to the country. We stayed in a small hotel. The weather was good. I didn't work.

Ritmo (di en voz alta):

- A: What did you **do** last **Sat**urday?
- B: I **went** to the **coun**try.
- A: Did you **go** **shop**ping last **week**?
- B: Yes, I **did**. I **bought** some **new** **shoes**.

Pares de sonido (elige un par y haz dos frases):

- *changed / travelled* — We changed trains. We travelled by bus.
- *answered / parked* — I answered the phone. I parked the car.
- *relaxed / played* — I relaxed at home. I played tennis.

Tres frases *the last time*:

- The last big city I visited was Madrid. I went there in May.
- The last film I saw was a French film. I saw it on Friday. I didn't like it.
- The last time I talked to a stranger was on a train. We talked about music.
