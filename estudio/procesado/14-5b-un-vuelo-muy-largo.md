## Qué vas a aprender

- Preguntar en presente simple con *I*, *you*, *we*, *they*: *Do you live in New York?*
- Responder corto: *Yes, I do. / No, I don't.*
- Usar frases verbales frecuentes: *live in a flat*, *watch TV*, *work for a company*.
- Hablar en un vuelo: dónde vives, familia, comida y hora de llegada.

## Explicación

La pregunta en presente simple con *I / you / we / they* se hace con *do*. El verbo principal queda en infinitivo: *Do you live here?* No *You live here?* ni *Live you here?*

Orden ASI: **A**uxiliar (*do*) + **S**ujeto (*you*, *they*…) + **I**nfinitivo (*live*, *have*…).

Las respuestas cortas repiten el auxiliar. No copies el verbo principal: *Yes, I do.* (no *Yes, I live.*) *No, I don't.*

| ? | + | − |
|---|---|---|
| Do I need a ticket? | Yes, you do. | No, you don't. |
| Do you live near here? | Yes, I do. | No, I don't. |
| Do we have good seats? | Yes, we do. | No, we don't. |
| Do they like children? | Yes, they do. | No, they don't. |

Eve (británica) y Wendy (estadounidense) van de Londres a Nueva York. En el avión hablan del libro, de dónde viven, de los hijos y del menú. Lee el diálogo y completa con: *book, children, meat, New York, pasta, time*.

Al final de las vacaciones Eve toma un taxi al aeropuerto. Lee el diálogo del taxi y marca lo que es verdad.

*w* /w/ (*want*, *work*, *when*) no es *v* /v/ (*very*, *have*, *live*, *TV*). En frases rápidas se enlazan palabras: *live_in*, *have_a*, *watch_a*.

## Vocabulario

**Frases verbales**

- **live in a flat / in a house** — vivir en un piso / en una casa
- **have breakfast / lunch / dinner** — desayunar / comer / cenar
- **watch TV** — ver la tele
- **listen to the radio / to music** — escuchar la radio / música
- **read the newspaper / a book** — leer el periódico / un libro
- **eat fast food / meat** — comer comida rápida / carne
- **drink tea / coffee / Coke / beer** — beber té / café / Coca-Cola / cerveza
- **speak English / French / German** — hablar inglés / francés / alemán
- **want a coffee / a ticket** — querer un café / un billete
- **have a dog / a cat / children** — tener un perro / un gato / hijos
- **like cats / this book** — gustar los gatos / este libro
- **work in a bank / for a company** — trabajar en un banco / para una empresa
- **study Spanish** — estudiar español
- **go to English classes / to the gym / to the cinema** — ir a clase de inglés / al gimnasio / al cine
- **need a new car / a ticket** — necesitar un coche nuevo / un billete

**Viaje**

- **a flight** — un vuelo
- **an attendant** — un/a auxiliar de vuelo
- **arrive** — llegar
- **the gate** — la puerta de embarque
- **traffic** — tráfico
- **expensive / cheap** — caro / barato
- **late** — tarde

Sonidos (di en voz alta):

- **/w/** (*witch*): want, work, when, where
- **/v/** (*vase*): very, have, live, TV

## Frases modelo

- Do you live in New York? No, I don't. I live in London.
- Do you like the book? Yes, I do. It's very good.
- Do you have children? No, we don't.
- Do you live in a flat? No, I don't. I live in a house.
- Do you have a big family? Yes, I do. I have three sisters.
- Do you watch a lot of TV? No, I don't. I read books.

En el vuelo (Eve y Wendy):

- Eve: Do you like the book?
- Wendy: Yes, I do. It's very good.
- Eve: She's my favourite writer. I love her books.
- Eve: Do you live in New York?
- Wendy: No, I don't. I live in London. My husband and I work for a British company.
- Eve: Oh! Do you have children?
- Wendy: No, we don't.
- Eve: I have two sons and a daughter. David and Andrew are at university and Carla's at school. Do you know Barbados?
- Wendy: No, I don't.
- Attendant: Do you want meat, fish, or pasta?
- Eve: Oh, fish, please.
- Wendy: Pasta for me, please.
- Eve: How's your pasta?
- Wendy: It's OK.
- Eve: This fish isn't very good. Excuse me, I don't like this fish. Can I have the pasta, please?
- Attendant: I'm sorry, madam. It's finished.
- Wendy: Excuse me. What time do we arrive?
- Attendant: In 25 minutes, madam.

En el taxi al aeropuerto (lee y elige):

- Driver: Where to?
- Eve: JFK, please. Not Newark.
- Driver: OK. The traffic is bad today.
- Eve: I'm from London. London is very expensive!
- Driver: I'm from Puerto Rico. I have two daughters.
- Eve: How much is it?
- Driver: $87.15. Have a good flight!
- Eve: Thanks. I'm not late. The gate is C5.
