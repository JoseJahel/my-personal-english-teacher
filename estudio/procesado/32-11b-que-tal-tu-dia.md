## Qué vas a aprender

- Pasado irregular de *get, go, have, do*: *got, went, had, did*.
- Frases hechas: *get up, go home, have lunch, do homework*.
- Preguntar *What time did you get up? Did you have breakfast?*
- Leer *Life in a Day* y escribir lo que hiciste ayer.

## Explicación

*get, go, have* y *do* no añaden *-ed*. Cambian de forma:

| Presente | Pasado |
|---|---|
| I get | I got |
| we go | we went |
| we have | we had |
| I do | I did |

Negación y pregunta: igual que los regulares, *didn't / Did* + **infinitivo**.

- *I didn't get up late.* Nunca *I didn't got up.*
- *Did you go to class?* Nunca *Did you went?*
- *Did you have breakfast?* Nunca *Did you had?*

*did* es dos cosas: auxiliar (*Did you go?*) y pasado de *do* (*I did my homework*).

Orden de pregunta:

- ASI: auxiliar + sujeto + infinitivo → *Did you get up early?*
- QuASI: palabra interrogativa + auxiliar + sujeto + infinitivo → *What time did you get up? Where did you go? What did you have? What did you do?*

| + | − |
|---|---|
| I got up late yesterday. | I didn't get up late. |
| He went to work by car. | He didn't go to work by car. |
| She had eggs for breakfast. | She didn't have eggs for breakfast. |
| We did yoga yesterday. | We didn't do yoga yesterday. |
| They did their homework. | They didn't do their homework. |

| Pregunta | Sí | No |
|---|---|---|
| Did you get up late? | Yes, I did. | No, I didn't. |
| Did he go to work by car? | Yes, he did. | No, he didn't. |
| Did they do their homework? | Yes, they did. | No, they didn't. |

Cada verbo arrastra nombres fijos. No digas *make homework* ni *take breakfast*: *do homework, have breakfast.*

Ben llega a casa de París. Pregunta a su hija Linda: *How was your day?* Ella no tuvo clase: museo, almuerzo, deberes. La madre *went out* — *I think she went shopping.*

## Vocabulario

**get**

- **get a flight** — coger un vuelo
- **get a taxi** — coger un taxi
- **get up (early)** — levantarse (temprano)
- **get home** — llegar a casa

**have**

- **have breakfast / lunch / dinner** — desayunar / almorzar / cenar
- **have a coffee** — tomar un café
- **have a shower** — ducharse
- **have a nice day** — pasar un buen día
- **have a (free) afternoon** — tener (libre) la tarde

**go**

- **go to bed** — acostarse
- **go home** — ir a casa
- **go out** — salir
- **go to school** — ir al colegio

**do**

- **do homework** — hacer los deberes
- **do housework** — hacer las tareas de casa
- **do sport** — hacer deporte
- **do yoga** — hacer yoga

Otras del día:

- **an early flight** — un vuelo temprano
- **an exam** — un examen
- **a noise** — un ruido
- **I'm back.** — Ya estoy.
- **How was your day?** — ¿Qué tal tu día?
- **pretty** — bonito
- **shave** — afeitarse
- **enjoy** — disfrutar
- **go to hospital** — ir al hospital
- **ask somebody to marry you** — pedirle matrimonio a alguien
- **get married** — casarse
- **have a party** — dar una fiesta
- **the 21st century** — el siglo XXI

## Frases modelo

Frases para memorizar (cierra los ojos y recita el verbo):

- get a flight / get a taxi / get up early / get home
- have breakfast / have a coffee / have lunch / have dinner / have a shower / have a nice day
- go to bed / go home / go out / go to school
- do homework / do housework / do sport / do yoga

Ben y Linda (completa con las letras dadas y luego copia el diálogo entero):

- Ben: Hi. I'm back. Linda! What are you doing at **home**?
- Linda: Hi, Dad. You're very early.
- Ben: Yes, I got an early **flight**.
- Linda: How was your day?
- Ben: My day was fine. But what about your day? Why aren't you at school?
- Linda: We didn't have **classes** today. We went to the **British** Museum in the morning. It was great. And then we had **lunch** in the café there.
- Ben: Why didn't you go to school this afternoon?
- Linda: We had a **free** afternoon. I stayed at home.
- Ben: What did you do?
- Linda: I did my **homework**. I had a lot of work.
- Ben: Good girl. When are your exams?
- Linda: They're next **week**.
- Ben: Where's your **mum**?
- Linda: She went out. I think she went **shopping**.
- Ben: What's that?
- Linda: What?
- Ben: That noise.

Ritmo (di en voz alta):

- A: What time did you **get** up?
- B: I **got** up at **7.30**.
- A: Did you have **break**fast?
- B: **Yes**, I **did**.
- A: What did you **have**?
- B: I had **toast** and **coff**ee.
- A: What did you **do** then?
- B: I **went** to **work**.

*Life in a Day* (Ridley Scott). El 24 de julio, el mismo día en 102 países, la gente filmó su vida. 4.500 horas de vídeo.

Early in the morning in the USA, a baby opened his eyes and looked at his mother. 'Isn't he pretty?' she asked the camera. Around the world, people got up, and washed, and had breakfast. A 15-year-old boy shaved for the first time. He didn't enjoy it! A boy kissed his mother on her birthday.

The 24th of July was a Saturday, so a lot of people didn't go to work. They relaxed and went to the park with their family, or went shopping. But other people worked hard, or cooked and cleaned. Some children played – but others worked. One small boy cleaned shoes in the street. One woman went to hospital for an operation.

Another woman waited all evening to talk to her husband on Skype. He was a soldier thousands of miles from home.

For some people, the 24th of July was a special day. A man arrived in Kathmandu. He was on a cycle trip around the world. A woman did an incredible skydive. An unemployed man got his first job. A boy who was at university went home to see his father for the first time in three years. Another man asked his girlfriend to marry him. A man and a woman got married and had an incredible party.

This beautiful film shows us life around the world in the 21st century. Watch it! It's free on YouTube.
