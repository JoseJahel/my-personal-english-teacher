## Qué vas a aprender

- Usar *like / love / hate / don't like* + verbo *-ing*.
- Nombrar actividades: *running, cooking, shopping, travelling, cycling.*
- Hablar de gustos a solas o con amigos.
- Emparejar perfiles: *She likes… and he likes…*

## Explicación

Después de *like, love, don't like* y *hate* va **verbo + *-ing***: *I love cooking. I like going for a walk. I don't like studying. I hate getting up early.* No *I like cook.*

Escala, de más positivo a más negativo: **love → like → don't like → hate**.

Ortografía del *-ing*:

| Infinitivo | *-ing* | Regla |
|---|---|---|
| read, fly | reading, flying | + *ing* |
| cycle, drive | cycling, driving | quita la *e* + *ing* |
| swim, shop | swimming, shopping | vocal + consonante final → duplica la consonante + *ing* |

En una web de citas, seis perfiles. Relaciona por gustos: Isabella toca el piano y hace deporte, no le gusta viajar; Ryan corre y va en bici, odia volar. Angie ama comprar ropa y el cine; Luke ama ir de compras y ver películas. Adriana camina por la montaña y odia ir de compras; Daniel cocina y le gusta acampar.

En posts, la gente dice qué hace sola y qué hace con amigos: cocinar solo, cenar fuera con amigos, no viajar solo, ir de compras con alguien, bailar solo en casa, ver películas juntos, leer solo, pasear acompañado, conducir y cantar a solas en el coche.

Pregunta: *Do you like camping? No, I hate it. I like sleeping in a bed.* *What about you?* *Me too!*

*U* corta /ʊ/ (*book, cook, look, good*) frente a /uː/ (*too, food, soon, school*). *-ing* = /ɪŋ/ (*doing, going, swimming*).

**Gustos**

## Frases modelo

- Do you like camping? No, I hate it. I like sleeping in a bed.
- I love cooking. What about you? I like cooking, too.
- Do you like reading books? Yes, I like reading good books.
- Do you like cycling? Yes, I do. I love cycling!
- I love buying clothes. What about you? Me too! I hate camping.

Perfiles:

- Isabella: I love playing the piano. I like doing sport. I don't like travelling.
- Ryan: I love running and cycling. I like classical music. I hate flying.
- Angie: I love buying clothes. I like the cinema. I don't like reading.
- Daniel: I love cooking. I like camping. I don't like watching TV.
- Adriana: I love walking in the mountains. I like good food. I hate shopping.
- Luke: I love shopping. I like watching films. I don't like sport.

Sola o con amigos (completa al leer):

- Chris: I like cooking alone. I don't like having other people in the kitchen. But I like eating out with friends, especially when I'm out. I don't like being alone in a restaurant.
- Sarah: I don't like travelling alone – I prefer going on holiday with friends. But they need to be the right friends!
- Mike: I like shopping with friends. When I go alone, I usually buy things that look horrible on me. I need a second opinion!

- Veronica: I like watching films with friends. When the film finishes, we can talk about it.

- Andy: I like driving alone. I love listening to my favourite music in the car and singing very loudly – but with the windows closed, of course!
