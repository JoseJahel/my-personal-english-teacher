## Qué vas a aprender

- Decir nacionalidades (*I'm Spanish*, *We're Canadian*).
- Usar *be* en plural: *we*, *you*, *they*.
- Distinguir *on holiday* y *on business*.
- Preguntar *Are you American?* y responder *No, we aren't. We're from Canada.*

## Explicación

El país es un nombre (*Spain*); la nacionalidad es un adjetivo (*Spanish*). *I'm from Spain* y *I'm Spanish* dicen casi lo mismo. El adjetivo de nacionalidad no cambia de género ni de número: *He's Spanish. She's Spanish. They're Spanish.*

*you* sirve para una persona y para varias. *they* son personas o cosas. *we* eres tú más otra gente.

En el pub *The King's Arms*, Jessica y Jim preguntan si hay sillas libres: *Are they free?* *free* aquí es «libres», no «gratis». *Please sit down.* Luego viene el origen: *Are you American? No, we aren't. We're from Canada.*

*on holiday* = de vacaciones. *on business* = de trabajo. Puedes estar de trabajo y tener *a free day* (un día libre).

| + | Contracción | − | Contracción |
|---|---|---|---|
| We are American. | We're American. | We are not American. | We aren't American. |
| You are Egyptian. | You're Egyptian. | You are not Egyptian. | You aren't Egyptian. |
| They are German. | They're German. | They are not German. | They aren't German. |

| Pregunta | Sí | No |
|---|---|---|
| Are we late? | Yes, you are. | No, you aren't. |
| Are you from Russia? | Yes, we are. | No, we aren't. |
| Are they Mexican? | Yes, they are. | No, they aren't. |

Orden: *They're from Russia.* → *Are they from Russia?*

Afirmación + nacionalidad a partir del país: *I'm from China.* → *He's Chinese.* / *I'm from Spain.* → *She's Spanish.*

## Frases modelo

- Are you American? No, we aren't. We're from Canada.

En *The King's Arms*:

- Jessica: Excuse me. Are they free?
- Richard: Yes, they are. Please sit down.
- Jessica: Thanks. I'm Jessica. Hi.
- Jim: And I'm Jim.
- Richard: Are you American?
- Jessica: No, we aren't. We're from Canada.
- Richard: Oh, OK! We're English. I'm Richard.
- Rachel: And I'm Rachel.
- Jim: Nice to meet you.

Más tarde:

- Jessica: Where in England are you from?
- Richard: We're from here, from Oxford.
- Jim: Oxford's a beautiful city!
- Rachel: Yes, it is. Are you on holiday?
- Jim: No, we aren't, we're on business. But today's a free day.
- Jessica: Yes, we're tourists today! Ooh. What's that?
- Jim: Oh… Are they your dogs?
- Richard: Yes, they are. Sit! Sit!
- Jessica: They're very nice. But I'm not very good with dogs.
- Jim: Look – a free table. Over there.
- Jessica: Nice to meet you. Have a nice day.
- Richard: Thanks. Nice to meet you, too.
- Rachel: Bye.
