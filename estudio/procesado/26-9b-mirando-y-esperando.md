## Qué vas a aprender

- Elegir *present simple* (rutina) o *present continuous* (ahora / hoy).
- Nombrar la ropa: *shirt, trousers, jacket, suit, jeans, coat*.
- Decir *I'm wearing…* y *I usually wear…*
- Leer sobre el día de un detective privado y describir qué lleva alguien.

## Explicación

El *present simple* habla de lo **habitual**: *I work in an office. I usually wear a shirt and trousers.* Palabras típicas: *always, usually, sometimes, never.*

El *present continuous* habla de **ahora, hoy, this week**: *Today I'm working in the street. I'm wearing sunglasses.*

Misma persona, dos tiempos:

- *He usually **works** in an office, but today he **is working** in the street.*
- *He usually **wears** a shirt and trousers. Today he **is wearing** a jacket and sunglasses, too.*

No mezcles las formas. *I work from home today* suena a rutina. Si es solo hoy: *I'm working from home today.* *Do you usually go to the office?* pregunta hábito. *Are you going to the office?* pregunta este momento.

*wear* = llevar puesto. *I'm wearing a black jacket* (ahora). *I usually wear jeans at the weekend.*

En inglés británico *trousers* es plural: *These trousers are black.* *jeans* también. *a suit* es chaqueta + pantalón a juego. *T-shirt* lleva guion.

Matt es detective privado. Un día típico es oficina, ordenador y llamadas. Hoy no: está en el coche, mira y espera. La vida real no es como en las películas. *solving a case is very satisfying*, pero esperar en un coche es *boring*.

## Frases modelo

Del artículo *A day in the life of a private investigator*:

- On a typical day, I get up early and I go to my office.
- I don't need to wear a suit. I usually wear a shirt and trousers.
- I start work at about 9.00. I sit at my desk and work at my computer.
- Then at about 6.00, I finish work and go home.
- Today I'm not working in the office. I'm working in the street.
- I'm sitting in my car with my camera. I'm watching and waiting.
- I'm wearing a jacket, and I'm wearing sunglasses because I don't want the woman to see my face.
- Waiting in a car is often boring, and it's a long day, not just 9.00 till 6.00.

Regla:

- Use the present simple to talk about **every day**.
- Use the present continuous to talk about **today / now**.
