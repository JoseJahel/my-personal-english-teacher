## Qué vas a aprender

- Decir qué está pasando ahora con el *present continuous* (*I'm making dinner*).
- Preguntar *What are you doing?* y *What's she doing?*
- Usar frases de viaje: *stay in a hotel*, *wait for a bus*, *pack a suitcase*.
- Leer mensajes y describir una escena: quién hace qué en este momento.

## Explicación

El *present continuous* sirve para lo que ocurre **ahora**, no para la rutina. Se forma con *be* + verbo + *-ing*: *I'm staying in a hotel. It's raining. They're playing a video game.*

No digas *I drive* si estás al volante en este instante: *I can't talk now. I'm driving.*

Contracciones: *I am* → *I'm*, *you are* → *you're*, *he is* → *he's*, *she is* → *she's*, *it is* → *it's*, *we are* → *we're*, *they are* → *they're*. Negación: *I'm not working. She isn't having a shower. They aren't listening.*

Pregunta: el verbo *be* va delante del sujeto. *You're working.* → *Are you working?* Con *What* / *Where*: *What are you doing? Where's he going?*

Respuestas cortas: *Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.* No contraigas el *yes*: no *Yes, I'm.*

| + | − |
|---|---|
| I'm working. | I'm not working. |
| You're sitting in my chair. | You aren't sitting in my chair. |
| He's playing football. | He isn't playing football. |
| She's having a shower. | She isn't having a shower. |
| It's raining. | It isn't raining. |
| We're having dinner. | We aren't having dinner. |
| They're listening. | They aren't listening. |

| Pregunta | Sí | No |
|---|---|---|
| Are you working now? | Yes, I am. | No, I'm not. |
| Is she having a shower? | Yes, she is. | No, she isn't. |
| Are they listening? | Yes, they are. | No, they aren't. |

Orden: sujeto + *be* + verbo *-ing*. Pregunta: *be* + sujeto + verbo *-ing*. *Wh-*: *Where are you going? What's he doing?*

Ortografía de *-ing*: *make* → *making* (cae la *e*); *have* → *having*; *sit* → *sitting* (consonante doble); *play* → *playing*.

Carol llama a casa desde Zúrich. Tony dice que los niños leen y que él cocina pescado. En la casa, en realidad, los niños juegan a un videojuego, Lily está con el móvil y hay pizza en la mesa. El continuous describe **lo que ves ahora**, no lo que alguien afirma.

## Vocabulario

- **What are you doing?** — ¿Qué estás haciendo?
- **I'm playing a video game.** — Estoy jugando a un videojuego.
- **do homework** — hacer los deberes
- **make dinner** — preparar la cena
- **watch a film / watch TV** — ver una película / ver la tele
- **read a story** — leer un cuento
- **help** — ayudar
- **It's raining.** — Está lloviendo.
- **darling** — cariño
- **Are you sure?** — ¿Seguro?
- **Have a good day.** — Que tengas un buen día.
- **a living room** — un salón
- **the box office** — la taquilla
- **traffic** — tráfico
- **noise** — ruido
- **outside / inside** — fuera / dentro
- **towards** — hacia
- **half an hour** — media hora
- **See you in 20 minutes.** — Nos vemos en 20 minutos.

**Viaje (verb phrases)**

- **book tickets** — reservar entradas / billetes
- **pack a suitcase** — hacer la maleta
- **leave the house** — salir de casa
- **carry a suitcase** — llevar una maleta
- **wear sunglasses** — llevar gafas de sol
- **get a taxi / a train / a bus** — coger un taxi / un tren / un autobús
- **wait for a flight** — esperar un vuelo
- **rent a car** — alquilar un coche
- **arrive at a hotel** — llegar a un hotel
- **stay in a hotel** — alojarse en un hotel
- **phone home** — llamar a casa
- **buy presents** — comprar regalos

Sonidos (di las palabras en voz alta, acentuando las sílabas fuertes):

- What are you **do**ing? I'm **mak**ing **din**ner.
- Are the **chil**dren **do**ing their **home**work?
- No, they **aren't**. They're **watch**ing **TV**.

## Frases modelo

Carol llama desde Suiza. Lee el diálogo y subraya lo que Tony dice y no es verdad (mira la escena: videojuego, móvil, pizza).

- Carol: Hello? Tony?
- Tony: Oh, hi, darling. How's Zurich?
- Carol: Fine, fine. I'm staying in a nice hotel – but it's raining. Can you hear it?
- Tony: Yes, yes, I can.
- Carol: How are the children?
- Tony: Oh, they're fine.
- Carol: Where are they? What are they doing?
- Tony: Lily's in the living room. She's doing her homework.
- Carol: And Matt and Josh?
- Tony: They're in their bedroom. They're reading.
- Carol: I think I can hear them. Are you sure they're reading?
- Tony: Yes, yes. That noise is the TV. Your mother's watching a film.
- Carol: What about you? Are you OK?
- Tony: I'm fine. I'm making dinner – fish and vegetables.
- Carol: Oh great! Very healthy. OK darling, I need to go now. See you on Tuesday.
- Tony: Have a good day tomorrow. Bye.

Ritmo (di en voz alta):

- A: What are you doing?
- B: I'm making dinner.
- A: Are the children doing their homework?
- B: No, they aren't. They're watching TV.
- A: What's your mother doing?
- B: She's helping me.

Mensajes de Mike y Lina (van al cine). Empareja 1–5 con A–E.

1. Hi. I'm just leaving the house now.
2. No, I'm not. I'm walking. See you in 20 minutes?
3. Where are you? I'm at the cinema, but I can't see you. I'm waiting outside.
4. It's really cold outside. I'm going in.
5. I'm standing near the box office. I'm wearing a black jacket. Can you see me?

- A: I'm arriving at the cinema now. Where are you?
- B: Me too. I'm walking to the bus stop. Are you getting the bus, too?
- C: Sorry, we're in a lot of traffic. There in five minutes.
- D: OK. See you then.
- E: Yes, I can! Can you see me? I'm walking towards you now!

Cuatro diálogos impresos de viaje:

1. En una tienda: *A: What are you buying? B: A football shirt for Johnny and a T-shirt for Jessica.*
2. En el hotel: *A: Is the hotel near the beach? B: No, it isn't. We're waiting for a bus.*
3. En el mostrador de coches: *A: I'd like to rent a car. A small manual car for three days. B: OK.*
4. En la parada: *A: Is this a number 23? B: No. The first bus is a 25. We're waiting for a number 13.*
