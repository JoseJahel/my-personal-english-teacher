## Qué vas a aprender

- Repasar el orden de las preguntas, el imperativo y los pronombres de objeto.
- Repasar *can / can't* y *like / love / hate + -ing*.
- Repasar tiempo libre, fechas, meses y actividades.
- Leer una guía de Belfast y decir qué se *puede* hacer allí.

## Explicación

Este bloque junta 7A, 7B, Inglés práctico 4, 8A y 8B. Sin audio: lees, escribes y dices en voz alta.

Puntos rápidos:

- Preguntas con *be*: verbo + sujeto. Con otros verbos: *(What) do/does + sujeto + infinitivo.*
- Imperativo: *Don't be late.* (no *Not be late.*) Objeto: *help us* (no *help our*); *I don't like him*; *I love them* (zapatos).
- *Can I park here?* (no *Do I can*). *you can't take photos* (no *can't to take*). Respuesta: *No, they can't* (no *don't* con *can*).
- *like / love / hate + reading, getting up, studying* (ortografía: *getting*, no *geting*; *studying*, no *studing*).

Lee *Come to Belfast and be inspired!* Empareja titulares: *St George's Market*, *The Titanic museum*, *World-class golf*. Luego marca si el texto *contiene* cada dato (no si te parece verdadero por el mundo real).

Autocomprobación: tiempo libre, órdenes (*Sit down*), cine, fechas, *can / can't* en tu ciudad, actividades que amas u odias.

Acento: *re|lax*, *thir|tieth*, *Jan|u|ary*, *Ju|ly*, *se|ven|teenth*, *sing|le*.

## Frases modelo

Gramática (frase correcta):

- A: Do you do any sport or exercise? B: No, I hate sport.
- What music do you like?
- Where's your mother from?
- Does your sister eat meat?
- The meeting's at 6.00. Don't be late.
- We're lost. Please help us.
- My brother has a new girlfriend, but I don't like him very much.
- They're beautiful shoes. I love them.
- Can I park here?
- Sorry, you can't take photos here.
- A: Can they come to dinner tomorrow? B: No, they can't.
- Can Lisa sit here?
- Do you like reading?
- I don't like getting up early.
- I hate studying at the weekend.

Belfast (lee y relaciona):

- When you are on holiday in the UK, don't forget to visit Belfast in Northern Ireland. If you like eating out, visiting places of interest, or doing sport or exercise, we have something for everybody!
- 1 St George's Market. This is one of Belfast's oldest attractions. Here you can try all kinds of different local food, and if you like cooking, you can buy wonderful products to take home. You can also listen to live music. The market is open on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.
- 2 The Titanic museum. The museum is situated next to the place where this great ship was made, just ten minutes from the city centre. You can learn all about the history of the Titanic, from its construction to its tragic end. You can also have lunch (but not dinner) at one of the museum's luxury restaurants, which are copies of the restaurants on the Titanic.
- 3 World-class golf. Northern Ireland is famous for its golfers, like Rory McIlroy and Darren Clarke. Belfast has 14 golf courses and the famous courses of Royal County Down and Royal Portrush are only an hour from Belfast by car.
