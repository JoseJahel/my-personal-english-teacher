## Qué vas a aprender

- Nombrar trabajos y lugares: *She's a journalist. She works for a newspaper.*
- Usar el presente simple con *he*, *she*, *it* (*works*, *doesn't*, *Does she…?*).
- Preguntar *What does she do?* y *Where does he work?*
- Dar razones con *Why…? Because…*

## Explicación

Con *he / she / it* el presente simple añade *-s* al verbo: *He works. She teaches. It works.*

La negación es *doesn't* (*does not*) + infinitivo: *She doesn't wear glasses.* No *She doesn't wears.*

La pregunta usa *Does*: *Does he work?* Orden ASI otra vez: *Does* + sujeto + infinitivo.

*have*, *go* y *do* son irregulares: *has*, *goes*, *does*.

| | I / you | he / she / it |
|---|---|---|
| ? | What do you do? | What does she do? |
| + | I work in a school. I have a good job. | He works in a school. He has a good job. |
| − | I don't wear glasses. | She doesn't wear glasses. |

Ortografía de la *-s*:

| Infinitivo | *he / she* | Regla |
|---|---|---|
| work, live | works, lives | + *s* |
| watch, finish | watches, finishes | + *es* (tras *ch, sh, s, ss, x*) |
| study | studies | consonante + *y* → *ies* |

Un trabajo lleva *a / an*: *She's a teacher. He's an actor.* El lugar: *in a hospital, in an office, in a school, from home, in the street.* *work for* + empresa: *She works for a newspaper.*

*What do you do?* = ¿A qué te dedicas? *Where do you work?* = ¿Dónde trabajas?

*Why…?* pide motivo. *Because…* lo da: *Why are the tourists happy? Because they can talk to Marcos in English.*

Marcos es camarero en Madrid: habla inglés todos los días con turistas. Charlotte trabaja en una oficina en París, en una multinacional: recibe visitas, contesta el teléfono y las reuniones son en inglés.

## Vocabulario

**Trabajos**

- **a teacher** — un/a profesor/a
- **a doctor** — un/a médico/a
- **a nurse** — un/a enfermero/a
- **a journalist** — un/a periodista
- **a waiter** — un camarero (*waitress* = camarera)
- **a shop assistant** — un/a dependiente/a
- **a receptionist** — un/a recepcionista
- **a police officer** — un/a policía
- **a factory worker** — un/a obrero/a de fábrica
- **a taxi driver** — un/a taxista
- **a lawyer** — un/a abogado/a
- **a chef** — un/a chef
- **a student** — un/a estudiante
- **unemployed** — en paro
- **retired** — jubilado/a

**Lugares de trabajo**

- **in a hospital** — en un hospital
- **in a shop** — en una tienda
- **in a restaurant** — en un restaurante
- **in an office** — en una oficina
- **in a school** — en un colegio
- **in a factory** — en una fábrica
- **from home** — desde casa
- **in the street** — en la calle
- **at university** — en la universidad
- **at school** — en el colegio (como alumno/a)

**Otras**

- **work for a company** — trabajar para una empresa
- **wear glasses** — llevar gafas
- **customers** — clientes
- **a multinational company** — una multinacional
- **welcome** — recibir, dar la bienvenida
- **Why? / Because…** — ¿Por qué? / Porque…

Tercera persona *-es* (di en voz alta): *does, finishes, goes, likes, lives, teaches, watches, writes.*

## Frases modelo

- What does she do? She's a journalist. She works for a newspaper.
- What do you do? Where do you work?
- He's a nurse. He works in a hospital. Does he speak English at work? No, he doesn't. Does he like his job? Yes, he does.

Un enfermero:

- A: What does he do?
- B: He's a nurse.
- A: Where does he work?
- B: He works in a hospital.
- A: Does he speak English at work?
- B: No, he doesn't.
- A: Does he like his job?
- B: Yes, he does.

Marcos (Madrid):

- I work in a restaurant in Madrid. I'm a waiter. I speak English at work every day, because a lot of tourists come here. I help customers with the menu and I say what the special dishes are. They are very happy because they can talk to me in English. A lot of tourists don't speak Spanish, but they usually speak English.

Charlotte (París):

- I'm a receptionist and I work in an office in Paris. It's a multinational company. When people from other countries visit the company, I need to welcome them in English. I also need to answer the phone in English. When we have meetings, we all speak in English, because it's the language of the company.

Modelo de texto:

- My mother is a teacher. She works at a primary school in São Paulo. She doesn't speak English at work. She loves her job because she likes children!
