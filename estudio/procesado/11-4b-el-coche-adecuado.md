## Qué vas a aprender

- Colores y adjetivos frecuentes: *small, fast, expensive, beautiful*…
- Colocar el adjetivo: *It's a beautiful car* (no *a car beautiful*).
- El adjetivo no cambia de número ni de género: *good cars*, no *goods cars*.
- Decir qué coche (o cosa) prefieres y por qué.

## Explicación

El adjetivo describe. En inglés va **delante** del nombre: *It's a fast car.* No *It's a car fast.* Detrás de *be* va solo: *An Audi is expensive. It's fast.*

El adjetivo es invariable: *It's an old house. They're old houses.* (no *olds houses*). Igual para hombre y mujer: *He's tall. She's tall, too.*

En el concesionario contrastan dos coches. El azul: *small*, *easy to park*, *slow*, *ugly*, *electric*, *eco-friendly*. El rojo: *sports car*, *fast*, *expensive*, *beautiful*. La madre y el hijo no están de acuerdo: *I prefer that red car.*

*I prefer small cities. / Me too. / I prefer big cities.* sirve para cualquier par de adjetivos.

Enlace (*linking*): la consonante final se une a la vocal siguiente: *a big umbrella*, *an old man*, *an expensive watch*.

| Bien | Mal |
|---|---|
| It's a beautiful car. | It's a car beautiful. |
| They're good cars. | They're goods cars. |
| They're old houses. | They're olds houses. |

## Vocabulario

**Colores**

- **blue / red / green / yellow** — azul / rojo / verde / amarillo
- **black / white / brown / orange / pink** — negro / blanco / marrón / naranja / rosa

**Adjetivos**

- **big / small** — grande / pequeño
- **fast / slow** — rápido / lento
- **expensive / cheap** — caro / barato
- **old / new** — viejo / nuevo
- **long / short** — largo / corto
- **beautiful / ugly** — bonito / feo
- **good / bad** — bueno / malo
- **easy** — fácil
- **electric / eco-friendly** — eléctrico / ecológico
- **perfect** — perfecto

**Marcas (nacionalidad)**

- **Jaguar** — English (or American)
- **Mercedes** — German
- **Ferrari** — Italian
- **Tesla** — American
- **Peugeot** — French
- **Honda** — Japanese

Sonidos:

- **/ɑː/** (*car*): fast, father, park, garden, are
- **/ɔː/** (*horse*): short, sport, small, awful

## Frases modelo

- Is it a good car? No, it isn't. It's small and very slow.

En el concesionario:

- Salesman: Is the car for you, sir?
- Woman: No, it's for me.
- Man: Yes, it's for my mother.
- Salesman: Well madam, what about this blue car here? It's small and it's easy to park.
- Man: Yes, Mum, it's perfect for you.
- Woman: But it's very slow. And it's ugly.
- Salesman: It's an electric car, madam. Very eco-friendly. They're good cars.
- Woman: I prefer… that red car.
- Man: But Mum, it's a sports car! It's very fast. And it's very expensive.
- Woman: Yes, but it's my money. It's a beautiful car and I love it! How much is it?
- Salesman: Come with me, madam.
- Man: Mum! …

Opiniones:

- My car is a Peugeot 208. It's French. It's small and it's green. It isn't very fast.
- I prefer small cities. / Me too. / I prefer big cities.
- They're black cats.

Pares para hablar: *small / big cities*; *Japanese / Mexican food*; *British / American films*; *cheap / expensive restaurants*; *old / new houses*; *long / short books*; *big / small dogs*; *black and white / colour photos*.
