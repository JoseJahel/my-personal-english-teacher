## Qué vas a aprender

- Repasar *a/an*, plurales, *this/that/these/those* y adjetivos.
- Cerrar familia, objetos pequeños, recuerdos, colores y opuestos.
- Leer textos sobre familias y extraer datos.
- Comprobar si puedes describir cosas, presentar parientes y pedir precios.

## Explicación

Files 3 y 4 se sostienen en tres piezas: nombrar objetos, señalarlos (*this/that/these/those*) y decir de quién son (*my, his, Sarah's*).

Errores típicos que este repaso caza:

- *an* delante de sonido vocálico: *an email*, *an umbrella*.
- Plural: *watches, cities, children, people* (no *watchs, citys, childs, persons*).
- Demostrativos: cerca/singular *this*; cerca/plural *these*; lejos/singular *that*; lejos/plural *those*.
- Adjetivo delante del nombre e invariable: *a fast car*, *good photos* (no *a car fast*, *goods photos*).
- Posesivo del dueño: *His name is Ken.* *Our son*. *Sophie's brother*. *My wife's name*.

Lee los dos textos impresos (Joseph Fisher y Claire) y responde con una frase completa. Luego escribe el tuyo con el mismo molde.

## Vocabulario

**Objetos**

- **a wallet / a bank card / a teddy** — cartera / tarjeta / osito
- **an umbrella / a key / a cap** — paraguas / llave / gorra
- **sunglasses / a key ring / a T-shirt** — gafas de sol / llavero / camiseta

**Familia (parejas)**

- **man — woman**
- **father — mother**
- **husband — wife**
- **son — daughter**
- **brother — sister**
- **boyfriend — girlfriend**
- **parents** = mother + father
- **children** = child × varios
- **men / women / people** — hombres / mujeres / personas

**Colores y opuestos**

- **white, blue, green, red, yellow, black, pink**
- **fast ≠ slow · big ≠ small · expensive ≠ cheap**
- **long ≠ short · new ≠ old · ugly ≠ beautiful**

**En un café o tienda**

- **How much is this…? / How much are those…?**
- **Can I have…, please?**

## Frases modelo

Joseph Fisher (texto impreso):

- My name's Joseph Fisher and I'm from Liverpool, in the UK. I'm married to Beverley and I have two children, a son and a daughter. My son's name is Brandon. He's 17. He's short with dark hair. My daughter's name is Grace. She's 19. I think my children are good-looking, probably because their mother is beautiful!

Claire (texto impreso):

- My name's Claire and I'm from Nantes in France. I'm 22. I have two sisters. Their names are Anne and Louise. Anne is 24. She's good-looking, with long blonde hair. She isn't married. Louise is 26 and very different from Anne. She's short with very dark hair. She's married. Her husband's name is Marius.
