## Qué vas a aprender

- Preguntar y dar direcciones: *Excuse me. Is there a bank near here?*
- Preposiciones: *next to, opposite, between, on the corner, on the left, on the right*.
- Seguir instrucciones: *Turn left. Turn right. Go straight on.*
- Ser educado: *Excuse me… Thanks very much. You're welcome.*

## Explicación

En la calle se empieza siempre con *Excuse me.* Luego *Is there a… near here?* Si hay uno: *Yes, there's one on the corner of King Street and East Road.*

*one* evita repetir el nombre: *Is there a bank? Yes, there's one opposite the gym.*

Preposiciones de lugar:

- **next to** = al lado
- **opposite** = enfrente
- **between** = entre (A **and** B)
- **on the corner** = en la esquina
- **on the left / on the right** = a la izquierda / a la derecha

Dirección en movimiento:

- *Go straight on.* = sigue todo recto
- *Turn left.* = gira a la izquierda
- *Turn right.* = gira a la derecha
- *Turn left when you leave the station.* = al salir de la estación, gira a la izquierda

Cierre: *Thanks very much. / Thank you. You're welcome.*

Si algo no funciona: *Excuse me, this cash machine isn't working.* Sorpresa: *I don't believe it!*

Intonación educada: sube un poco en *Excuse me* y en la pregunta. Di en voz alta, sin precipitarte:

- **Ex**cuse me. Is there a **bank** near **here**?
- **Yes**, there's **one** on the **cor**ner of **King** Street and **East** Road.
- **Thank** you.

*cash machine* = cajero. En Londres el *Tube* y el *Heathrow Express* conectan el aeropuerto con Paddington.

## Vocabulario

**Preposiciones**

- **next to** — al lado de
- **opposite** — enfrente de
- **between** — entre
- **on the corner** — en la esquina
- **on the left** — a la izquierda
- **on the right** — a la derecha

**Movimiento**

- **Turn left. / Turn right.** — Gira a la izquierda. / a la derecha.
- **Go straight on.** — Sigue todo recto.
- **when you leave the station** — al salir de la estación

**Sitios del plano**

- **a bank** — un banco
- **a cash machine** — un cajero
- **a hospital** — un hospital
- **a train station** — una estación de tren
- **a hotel** — un hotel
- **a car park** — un aparcamiento
- **a gym** — un gimnasio
- **a school** — un colegio
- **a factory** — una fábrica
- **an Italian restaurant / a Chinese restaurant** — un restaurante italiano / chino
- **a cinema** — un cine
- **a supermarket** — un supermercado
- **a bookshop** — una librería
- **a coffee shop** — una cafetería
- **a phone shop** — una tienda de móviles
- **a park** — un parque

**Frases útiles**

- **Excuse me. Is there a bank near here?** — Perdone, ¿hay un banco cerca?
- **Yes, there's one…** — Sí, hay uno…
- **Thanks very much.** — Muchas gracias.
- **You're welcome.** — De nada.
- **I don't believe it!** — ¡No me lo puedo creer!
- **This cash machine isn't working.** — Este cajero no funciona.
- **It only takes about 15 minutes.** — Solo tarda unos 15 minutos.

Calles del plano: *East Road, North Street, London Road, School Road, King Street, South Street.*

## Frases modelo

Plano (norte arriba): hospital (NO) y *train station* (N); *car park* y *bank* + *gym* en el centro; *hotel* (NE); *school* y parque (E); *factory* e *Italian restaurant* (SO); *cinema* y *supermarket* + *bookshop* (S).

Diálogos cortos sobre el plano:

- A: Excuse me. Is there a bank near here?
- B: Yes, there's one on the corner of King Street and East Road. It's between the car park and the gym.
- A: Thank you.

- A: Excuse me. Is there a supermarket near here?
- B: Yes. Go straight on. It's on the right, next to the bookshop.
- A: Thanks very much.
- B: You're welcome.

- A: Is there a hospital near here?
- B: Yes, there's one on East Road, opposite the car park.

Rob pide un cajero:

- Rob: Excuse me. Is there a cash machine near here?
- Woman: Yes. There's one at Barclays Bank.
- Rob: Sorry, where is that?
- Woman: Turn left and go straight on. Then turn right and it's on the right.
- Rob: Thanks very much.
- Woman: You're welcome.

Jenny llama a Rob. Hotel **Indigo**, **London** Street, cerca de **Paddington Station**. *Get the Heathrow Express train from the airport to Paddington. It only takes about 15 minutes.*

Direcciones al hotel:

- Turn **left** when you leave the station.
- Then go **straight on** for a bit and turn **right** into London Street.
- The hotel's **opposite** Norfolk Square.
