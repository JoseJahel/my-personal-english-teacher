## Qué vas a aprender

- Nombrar países y decir de dónde es una persona o una cosa.
- Usar *be* con *he*, *she* e *it* (afirmación, negación y pregunta).
- Distinguir *he* (hombre), *she* (mujer) e *it* (cosa, ciudad, música).
- Preguntar *Where's he from?* y responder *He's from Brazil.*

## Explicación

*Where are you from?* pregunta el origen. La respuesta corta es *I'm from* + país o ciudad. Si la ciudad no es famosa, sitúala: *Where's Toledo? It's in Spain.*

Con tercera persona cambia el verbo: *he is* → *he's*, *she is* → *she's*, *it is* → *it's*. En la pregunta: *Is she from Mexico?* Respuestas cortas: *Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.* No contraigas el *yes*: *Yes, he is* (no *Yes, he's*).

*he* = hombre, *she* = mujer, *it* = cosa, ciudad, instrumento o país. *Where's it from?* sirve para un instrumento o una canción.

Los nombres de país van con mayúscula: *Brazil*, no *brazil*. El artículo va en *the United States* y *the UK*.

| | Forma plena | Contracción |
|---|---|---|
| + | He is from Italy. She is from Spain. It is from China. | He's / She's / It's |
| − | He is not from Egypt. She is not from Brazil. It is not from Japan. | He isn't / She isn't / It isn't |

| Pregunta | Sí | No |
|---|---|---|
| Is he from France? | Yes, he is. | No, he isn't. |
| Is she from Poland? | Yes, she is. | No, she isn't. |
| Is it good? | Yes, it is. | No, it isn't. |

Cuadro de *be* singular:

| + | − |
|---|---|
| I am = I'm | I am not = I'm not |
| you are = you're | you are not = you aren't |
| he is = he's | he is not = he isn't |
| she is = she's | she is not = she isn't |
| it is = it's | it is not = it isn't |

Orden: *She's from Russia.* → *Is she from Russia?* Con *What* y *Where*: *What's your name? Where are you from? Where's he from?*

Negación: *He is not* = *He isn't* o *He's not*.

## Vocabulario

- **Where's he from?** — ¿De dónde es él?
- **He's from Brazil.** — Es de Brasil.
- **country / city** — país / ciudad
- **Brazil** — Brasil
- **China** — China
- **Egypt** — Egipto
- **England / the UK** — Inglaterra / el Reino Unido
- **France** — Francia
- **Germany** — Alemania
- **Italy** — Italia
- **Japan** — Japón
- **Mexico** — México
- **Nigeria** — Nigeria
- **Poland** — Polonia
- **Russia** — Rusia
- **Spain** — España
- **Switzerland** — Suiza
- **Türkiye** — Turquía
- **the United States (the USA)** — los Estados Unidos
- **he / she / it** — él / ella / eso (cosa)
- **I don't know.** — No lo sé.

Sonidos (di las palabras en voz alta):

- **/ɪ/** (*fish*): it, Italy, six, England
- **/əʊ/** (*phone*): no, don't, Poland, Mexico, know
- **/s/** (*snake*): say, seven, city, nice
- **/ʃ/** (*shower*): she, Russia

## Frases modelo

- A: Where are you from?
- B: I'm from Toledo.
- A: Where's Toledo?
- B: It's in Spain.

Cartel de un festival (texto impreso): *World Music Festival, June 18–19. Lucas Santtana, Friday 18. Lila Downs, Saturday 19.*

- A: Wow! Lucas Santtana!
- B: Where's he from?
- A: He's from Brazil.
- B: Is Lila Downs from Brazil, too?
- A: No, she isn't. She's from Mexico.
- B: Is she good?
- A: Yes, she is. Very good.

Preguntas sobre fotos:

- Where's he from? He's from the USA.
- Where's she from? She's from Japan.
- Where's it from? It's from Spain.
- Number two. Is she from China? No, she isn't. She's from Japan.
