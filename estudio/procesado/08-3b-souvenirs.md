## Qué vas a aprender

- Nombrar recuerdos: *cap, mug, T-shirt, key ring*…
- Usar *this / that / these / those* según distancia y número.
- Preguntar el precio: *How much is it? / How much are they?*
- Distinguir *here*, *there* y *over there*.

## Explicación

*this / these* son para lo que tienes cerca (*here*). *that / those* son para lo que está lejos (*there* o *over there*).

| | aquí (*here*) | allí (*there*) |
|---|---|---|
| singular | **this** | **that** |
| plural | **these** | **those** |

*this / that* = una cosa. *these / those* = varias. También van con personas: *This is my brother. Who are those girls over there?*

Pueden ser pronombre o adjetivo: *This is my book.* (pronombre) / *This book is very nice.* (adjetivo).

En un puesto de souvenirs la pregunta clave es el precio. Singular: *How much is this mug?* Plural: *How much are these key rings?* La libra se escribe £ y se lee *pounds*.

El sonido /ð/ (*mother*) aparece en *this, that, these, those, the, they*. La lengua toca los dientes.

## Vocabulario

- **a souvenir** — un recuerdo (de viaje)
- **a cap** /kæp/ — una gorra
- **a football scarf** — una bufanda de fútbol
- **a football shirt** — una camiseta de fútbol
- **a key ring** — un llavero
- **a mug** — una taza
- **a plate** — un plato
- **a teddy** — un osito
- **a T-shirt** — una camiseta
- **How much is it / are they?** — ¿Cuánto cuesta / cuestan?
- **here / there / over there** — aquí / ahí / allí
- **this / that / these / those** — este / ese / estos / esos
- **pound (£)** — libra

Puesto (fotos numeradas del libro): 1 gorras, 2 llaveros, 3 camisetas de fútbol, 4 ositos, 5 tazas, 6 bufandas, 7 camisetas, 8 camisetas de equipo. Precios visibles en el puesto: *£2.99*, *£3.99*, *£4.11*, *£9.99*.

## Frases modelo

- What are those? They're key rings.

En el puesto:

- Woman: Excuse me. What are those?
- Man: They're T-shirts.
- Woman: How much are they?
- Man: They're £9.99.

- Woman: And how much are these key rings?
- Man: They're £2.99.
- Woman: And this mug?
- Man: £3.99.

- Woman: Is that a Manchester United shirt?
- Man: No, it's Arsenal.
- Woman: How much is it?
- Man: £9.99.
- Woman: Oh… no. Thank you. Bye.

Más modelos de precio:

- How much is this mug? It's £3.99.
- How much is that shirt? It's £9.99.
- How much are these key rings? They're £2.99.
- How much are those T-shirts? They're £9.99.
- Two caps, please. That's £6.00.
