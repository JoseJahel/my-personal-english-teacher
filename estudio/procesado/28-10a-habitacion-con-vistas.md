## Qué vas a aprender

- Decir lo que hay en un lugar: *there's a… / there are some…*
- Preguntar *Is there a restaurant? Are there any shops?*
- Vocabulario de hotel y habitación: *lift, view, wi-fi, pillow, towel*.
- Preposiciones de lugar: *in, on, under*.
- Distinguir *here / near / wear* frente a *there / chair*.

## Explicación

*There is / There are* presentan lo que hay en un sitio. No es *it is*. *There's a TV in the room* = en la habitación hay un televisor.

Singular: *there's a / an* + nombre. *There's a room on the second floor. There's a great view.*
Plural: *there are some* + nombre. *There are some pubs in the village.* *some* = algunos, sin número exacto.

Negación y pregunta con plural usan *any*, no *some*:

- *There aren't any shops near the hotel.*
- *Are there any shops near here?*

Singular: *There isn't a restaurant. Is there wi-fi?*

Respuestas cortas: *Yes, there is. / No, there isn't. Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.*

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| + | There's a TV. There's a shower. | There are two beds. There are some pictures. |
| − | There isn't a phone. There isn't a bath. | There aren't any towels. There aren't any books. |
| ? | Is there a TV? | Are there any pictures? |
| Sí / No | Yes, there is. / No, there isn't. | Yes, there are. / No, there aren't. |

En recepción, *lift* (Reino Unido) y *elevator* (EE. UU.) son el mismo aparato. En Escocia un *loch* es un lago. *Nessie* es el monstruo de Loch Ness: *some people say there isn't and some people say there is.*

*in, on, under* para objetos: la pelota *in* the box, *on* the box, *under* the box. El mando: *It's on the table. It's under the bed. It's in the bag.*

Misma grafía, distinto sonido:

- **/ɪə/** *ear*: *near, here, beer, dear, year, really, idea, airport*
- **/eə/** *chair*: *wear, there, their, where, repair*

*Here's your beer, dear. There's a chair over there.*

## Frases modelo

Texto turístico (lee y responde: ¿te gustaría ir a Escocia?):

Scotland is one of the world's top tourist destinations, with 10,000 km of coast, 790 islands, more than 30,000 'lochs', famous castles, and beautiful cities. There's something for visitors of all ages.

Loch Ness is 10 km south-west of the city of Inverness – perfect for day trips. You can take a boat trip on the loch and visit the Loch Ness Centre to find out all about 'Nessie', the monster. One of the most beautiful castles in the world, Urquhart Castle, is on the banks of the loch. There are wonderful places for walking and cycling very near.

Stay at the Craigdarroch Hotel. It's the perfect place for a great view of the loch.

En recepción (Louisa y Eric):

- Eric: Hello. Do you have a room for tonight?
- Receptionist: Let's see. Yes, there's a room on the second floor.
- Louisa: Great. Can we see it?
- R: Of course. Come with me.
- E: Is there an elevator?
- R: A lift? I'm sorry, no there isn't. But I can help you with your cases.
- R: This is the room.
- L: It's beautiful. I love it!
- E: Yes, and there's a great view of Loch Ness. Is there wi-fi?
- R: Yes, there is. The password is 'Scotland'.
- E: Is there a restaurant? We're very hungry.
- R: No, there isn't a restaurant. But you can have a sandwich in the bar, or there are some pubs in the village.
- L: Are there any shops near here?
- R: No, there aren't any shops near the hotel.
- E: Is there really a monster in Loch Ness?
- R: Well, some people say there isn't and some people say there is. Enjoy your stay. Breakfast is at 8.00.

El hotel: no hay ascensor, no hay restaurante, no hay tiendas cerca. Sí hay habitación con vistas, wifi y bar.

Mando a distancia:

- A: Where's the remote control?
- B: It's on the table. / It's under the pillow. / It's in the suitcase.

Aula: *There's a TV. There are some pictures. There aren't any windows.*
