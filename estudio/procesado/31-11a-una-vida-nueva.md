## Qué vas a aprender

- Pasado regular: *We booked our flights. We didn't want to sleep.*
- Preguntar *Did they invite their friends?* y responder *Yes, they did. / No, they didn't.*
- Ortografía *-ed* y pronunciación /d/, /t/, /ɪd/.
- Leer cómo Dominic y Miriam se mudan un año a Durham, Carolina del Norte.

## Explicación

El *past simple* regular añade *-ed* (o *-d*) al infinitivo. Vale para todas las personas: *I arrived, he arrived, they arrived.*

Negación: *didn't* + infinitivo **sin** *-ed*. *They didn't want to sleep.* Nunca *They didn't wanted.*

Pregunta: *Did* + sujeto + infinitivo. *Did you watch TV?* Nunca *Did you watched?*

Respuestas cortas: *Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. Yes, they did. / No, she didn't.*

| Presente | Pasado + |
|---|---|
| we book | We booked our flights. |
| we arrive | We arrived in North Carolina. |
| they don't want | They didn't want to sleep. |

| + | − |
|---|---|
| I arrived at seven o'clock. | I didn't arrive at seven o'clock. |
| You finished the book. | You didn't finish the book. |
| He wanted a coffee. | He didn't want a coffee. |
| She liked the film. | She didn't like the film. |
| We studied Spanish. | We didn't study Spanish. |
| They stopped at a café. | They didn't stop at a café. |

| Pregunta | Sí | No |
|---|---|---|
| Did you watch TV yesterday? | Yes, I did. | No, I didn't. |
| Did she walk to work? | Yes, she did. | No, she didn't. |
| Did they play tennis? | Yes, they did. | No, they didn't. |

Ortografía:

- verbo + *-ed*: *work → worked, finish → finished*
- verbo en *-e* + *-d*: *live → lived, change → changed, arrive → arrived*
- consonante + *y* → *ied*: *cry → cried, study → studied*
- consonante–vocal–consonante: doble + *-ed*: *stop → stopped, travel → travelled* (UK)

Pronunciación de *-ed* (di en voz alta):

- **/d/** *dog*: *arrived, played*
- **/t/** *tie*: *booked, looked, packed*
- **/ɪd/**: *rented, invited* (después de *t* o *d*)

Miriam es médica; Dominic, diseñador. Los dos trabajan en el Reino Unido. Tienen dos hijos, Sacha y Elena. La Universidad Duke, en Durham (North Carolina), ofrece a Miriam un año de trabajo. Al principio no están seguros: el colegio de Sacha, la casa… Al final *we decided to go.*

## Frases modelo

*We followed our dream* (lee y elige):

My wife and I always wanted to live abroad. But Miriam and I both work in the UK – she's a doctor and I'm a designer – and we have two small children, Sacha and Elena. Then an American university, Duke, in Durham, North Carolina, asked Miriam to go and work there for a year. At first, we weren't sure what to do. There were so many questions. What about Sacha's school? What do we do with our house? But finally, we decided to go!

1. Dominic and his wife wanted to live ______. a in another country b in another place in their country
2. Duke University offered ______ a job in the USA. a Dominic b Miriam
3. In the beginning, they ______ sure if they wanted to go. a were b weren't

Frases y fotos A–F (escribe la letra):

1. We rented a house near the university. (casa entre árboles)
2. We booked our flights. It was a long journey! (pantalla de reservas)
3. We invited our friends to our local pub to say goodbye. (mesa en un pub)
4. We packed seven heavy suitcases. (maletas)
5. The children didn't want to sleep on the plane. Sacha played with Lego and Elena looked at books. (avión)
6. When we arrived in North Carolina, we were very tired!

Preguntas *Did…?*

- Did Dominic and Miriam want to live abroad? Yes, they did.
- Did Duke University offer Dominic a job? No, it didn't.
- Did they invite their friends to their house? No, they didn't. They invited them to the pub.
- Did the children want to sleep on the plane? No, they didn't.
- Did Sacha play with Lego on the plane? Yes, he did.
- Did Elena play with Sacha? No, she didn't. She looked at books.

Blog en Durham:

At first, living in the USA was quite difficult. We missed our friends and family and we didn't like the food. It was difficult to find fresh fruit and vegetables. We also needed to rent a car. In London, we don't drive, but in Durham, not many people walk or use public transport!

But we soon started to enjoy it. Our house was nice, and near a lovely park. Sacha liked his school, especially his music teacher, who played the trumpet! He worked hard and his teachers were very happy with him.

Título posible del final: *We want to go back to the UK!* o *We want to go back to the USA!* Tú decides con lo que lees y escribes dos frases de motivo.

Fotos impresas del blog: Sacha in class; a trip to a blueberry farm; a trip to a National Park (*Northern water snake – habitat – please don't disturb the snakes*).
