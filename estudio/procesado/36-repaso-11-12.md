## Qué vas a aprender

- Repasar el *past simple*: regulares, irregulares y *was / were*.
- Evitar *Did you liked?* y *I didn't went.*
- Recitar preposiciones de lugar y el pasado de 20 verbos.
- Leer *A unique culture* (una estadounidense en Jordania) y decir lo que hiciste ayer.

## Explicación

Files 11 y 12 cierran el pasado. Antes de los ejercicios, tres filtros:

1. **Afirmación**: regular + *-ed* (*studied, not studyed*). Irregular de memoria (*went, had, got, did, bought, saw, came, left*). *be* = *was / were*.
2. **Negación**: *didn't* + infinitivo. *He didn't work. I didn't buy it. Maria didn't come.* Nunca *didn't worked / didn't bought / didn't came.*
3. **Pregunta**: *Did* + sujeto + infinitivo. *Did you like the film? What time did they arrive? Where did you go?* Con *be*: *Was the film good? What time did you go to bed?* no *Did you went.*

*What time did you get up this morning?* usa infinitivo *get up*, no *got up*.

*A Was the film good? B Yes, fantastic.* — aquí el verbo es *be*, por eso *Was*, no *Did*.

Preposiciones del plano: *opposite, next to, between, on the corner of, on the left, on the right.*

Acento: *WEAther, oPPOsite, beTWEEN, deCIDE, uniVERsity, exCHANGE.*

Una estudiante estadounidense pasó **tres meses** (no tres semanas) en la Universidad Al-Ahliyya de Amman. Menciones de lugar: **Amman**, **Wadi Rum**, **Petra**. Cada día era distinto de su vida en EE. UU. Estudió política de Oriente Medio, historia del mundo árabe y árabe. Recuerdos: camello en Wadi Rum, excursión a Petra, campo hermoso, gente amable. Recomendación: estudiar en un país completamente distinto y quedarse el máximo tiempo posible.

## Frases modelo

Gramática (elige *a* o *b*):

0. ____'s your name? a Who **b What**
1. They ____ English at school. **a studied** b studyed
2. ____ the film? **a Did you like** b Liked you
3. He ____ late yesterday. a doesn't work **b didn't work**
4. What time ____? **a did they arrive** b did they arrived
5. I ____ to the USA last year. a was **b went**
6. I ____ the homework last night. **a didn't do** b didn't
7. What time did you ____ this morning? **a get up** b got up
8. We ____ lunch at a great restaurant on Sunday. a haved **b had**
9. Where ____ on holiday? **a did you go** b you did go
10. A ____ the film good? B Yes, fantastic. a Did **b Was**
11. I liked the jacket, but I ____ it. **a didn't buy** b didn't bought
12. A What time ____ to bed last night? B At 10.15. a did you went **b did you go**
13. Alice ____ to Dublin last month. a goes **b went**
14. Maria ____ to class yesterday. **a didn't come** b didn't came
15. What time ____? a finished the concert **b did the concert finish**

*A unique culture* — lee entero:

I studied for three months at Al-Ahliyya Amman University, in Amman, the capital of Jordan. It was a life-changing experience. Every day was a new adventure – very different from my life in the USA. While I was there, I studied US politics in the Middle East, the history of the Arab World, and Arabic language.

People often ask me what my favourite memory is, but it's difficult to answer because there are so many! I loved riding a camel in the Wadi Rum desert and I went on an amazing excursion to Petra. The countryside was beautiful and completely different from where I live in the United States. I also enjoyed the unique culture of Jordan. Of course, there were things that I sometimes found difficult, but everybody from Jordan was very understanding and friendly.

My recommendation for people who want to study abroad is to go to a country that is completely different from where you live and to stay as long as you can. I loved learning about another culture and I think studying abroad is a great experience.
