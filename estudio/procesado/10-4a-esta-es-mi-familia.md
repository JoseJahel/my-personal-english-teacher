## Qué vas a aprender

- Nombrar personas y parientes: *husband, wife, son, daughter*…
- Usar adjetivos posesivos: *my, your, his, her, its, our, their*.
- Usar el genitivo *'s*: *She's my boyfriend's sister.*
- Presentar a la familia: *This is my husband, Mark.*

## Explicación

El posesivo concuerda con el *dueño*, no con la cosa. *His name* = el nombre de él. *Her name* = el nombre de ella. *Their names* = los nombres de ellos.

| Sujeto | Posesivo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| I | my | This is **my** husband, Mark. |
| you | your | **Your** number is in my phone. |
| he | his | **His** name's Oliver. |
| she | her | **Her** name's Emma. |
| it | its | What's **its** name? |
| we | our | These are **our** children. |
| you (pl.) | your | Sarah is **your** babysitter. |
| they | their | What are **their** names? |

*its* es de cosas o animales: *Its name is Chez Marcelle.* No lo confundas con *it's* (= *it is*): *It's a French restaurant.* / *Its name is Chez Marcelle.*

El *'s* de posesión va detrás de una persona: *Jack's car, Ben's wife, Maria's daughter.* En plural regular: *my parents' house* (apóstrofo después de la *s*).

Cuidado: *She's American.* (*'s* = *is*) frente a *Emma is Maria's daughter.* (*'s* = posesión). Pronunciación de *'s*: /s/ (*Jack's*), /z/ (*Maria's*), /ɪz/ tras nombre en *s* (*Carlos's*).

Plurales irregulares: *child → children*, *man → men*, *woman → women*, *person → people*. *mother + father = parents* (no *fathers*). *grandmother + grandfather = grandparents*.

/ə/ es la vocal átona de *-er*: *mother, daughter, sister*.

## Vocabulario

**Personas**

- **a boy / a girl** — un niño / una niña
- **a man / a woman** — un hombre / una mujer
- **children / friends** — niños / amigos

**Familia**

- **husband / wife** — marido / esposa
- **mother / father** — madre / padre
- **son / daughter** — hijo / hija
- **brother / sister** — hermano / hermana
- **grandfather / grandmother** — abuelo / abuela
- **boyfriend / girlfriend** — novio / novia
- **parents / grandparents** — padres / abuelos
- **partner** — pareja
- **babysitter** — canguro

Sonidos:

- **/ʌ/** (*up*): husband, Sunday, son, mother, brother
- **/æ/** (*cat*): man, family, bag, thanks, that
- **/ə/** (*computer*): mother, sister, daughter, children, woman

## Frases modelo

- Who's Selma? She's my boyfriend's sister.

Parte 1 (en la puerta):

- Maria: Hi, Sarah! Come in.
- Sarah: Thanks.
- Maria: This is my husband, Mark.
- Mark: Hello.
- Sarah: Hi.
- Maria: And these are our children.
- Children: Hello!
- Sarah: What are their names?
- Maria: Her name's Emma, and his name's Oliver.
- Emma: And this is our cat.
- Sarah: Ah! What's its name?
- Emma: Her name is Princess. She's a girl.
- Sarah: Oh, sorry.

Parte 2:

- Maria: The name of the restaurant is Mario's Bistro. The phone number's on the table over there.
- Sarah: Great, thanks.
- Maria: And my husband's phone number is there, too.
- Sarah: OK. And your number is in my phone.
- Maria: Now, children. Sarah is your babysitter. Be good.
- Children: OK, Mum.

Más:

- What's his name? What's her name?
- Who's Marco? He's my sister's husband.
- Is Justin your husband? No, he's my brother.
