## Qué vas a aprender

- Pasado de *be*: *I was, you were, he was, we were*.
- Preguntar *Where were you at seven o'clock?* y responder *I was at home.*
- Preposiciones de lugar en pasado: *at home, in bed, on a train*.
- Leer una entrevista policial y un texto sobre un edificio antiguo.

## Explicación

*was / were* es el pasado de *am / is / are*.

- *am / is* → *was*: *I was at home. He was tired. It was hot.*
- *are* → *were*: *You were in class. We were in London. They were famous.*

Negación: *wasn't* = *was not*; *weren't* = *were not*. *I wasn't at home. You weren't in class.*

Pregunta: cambia el orden. *You were late.* → *Were you late?* *She was a singer.* → *Was she a singer?*

Respuestas cortas: *Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't. Yes, they were. / No, they weren't.* No contraigas el *yes*.

El pasado de *there is* es *there was*. El de *there are* es *there were*. *There was a robbery in a bank. There were 42 rooms.*

| Presente | Pasado |
|---|---|
| I'm at home. | I was at home. |
| You aren't at home. | You weren't at home. |
| Are you alone? | Were you alone? |
| No, I'm not. | No, I wasn't. |

| + | − |
|---|---|
| I was at home at eight o'clock. | I wasn't at home. |
| You were in class yesterday. | You weren't in class. |
| He was tired last night. | He wasn't tired. |
| It was hot last week. | It wasn't hot. |
| We were in London last month. | We weren't in London. |
| They were famous in the 60s. | They weren't famous. |

| Pregunta | Sí | No |
|---|---|---|
| Were you late? | Yes, I was. | No, I wasn't. |
| Was she a singer? | Yes, she was. | No, she wasn't. |
| Were they in Mexico last week? | Yes, they were. | No, they weren't. |

Expresiones de pasado: *this morning, yesterday, last night, last week, last month, last year, yesterday afternoon, last Friday night.*

*in, on, at* con lugares:

| *at* | *in* | *on* |
|---|---|---|
| at home | in bed | on a bus |
| at work | in London | on a train |
| at school | in the kitchen | on a plane |
| at university | in the car | on the beach |
| | in the park / in the street | |
| | in a restaurant / a pub / a museum | |
| | in a shopping centre / a hotel | |

*at* = punto (casa, trabajo, colegio). *in* = interior o ciudad. *on* = transporte y *the beach*. *in bed*, no *on bed*.

Anoche hubo un robo en un banco. Jason Brown es sospechoso. El detective pregunta *Where were you at eight o'clock last night?*

## Vocabulario

- **Where were you?** — ¿Dónde estabas?
- **I was at home.** — Estaba en casa.
- **a robbery** — un robo
- **a bank** — un banco
- **a suspect** — un sospechoso
- **a detective** — un detective
- **alone** — solo/a
- **yesterday / yesterday afternoon** — ayer / ayer por la tarde
- **last night / last Friday night** — anoche / el viernes por la noche
- **secret / strong / together** — secreto / fuerte / juntos
- **a building** — un edificio
- **a luxury hotel** — un hotel de lujo
- **a cellar** — un sótano
- **a vault** — una cámara acorazada
- **lock** — cerrar con llave
- **store rooms** — trasteros / almacenes

Sonidos (di en voz alta, copiando el ritmo):

- Where **were** you at **sev**en o'**clock** **yes**terday **morn**ing?
- I was at **home**.
- Were you in **bed**? **Yes**, I **was**.
- Were you **there** all **morn**ing? **No**, we **weren't**.

## Frases modelo

Entrevista impresa (Jason y el detective):

- Detective: Where were you at eight o'clock last night?
- Jason: I was at home.
- Detective: You weren't at home. Your wife says you were out. She was at home alone.
- Jason: Oh yes, I remember now. I was in a restaurant.
- Detective: Were you alone?
- Jason: No, I wasn't. I was with a friend. Kevin Smith. You can ask him.

Kevin, al día siguiente (texto para leer):

Name: Kevin Smith. Job: journalist. Address: 15 Market Street.
Kevin knows Jason because they were at school together.
At 5.00 yesterday afternoon, he was in his office.
At 7.00, he was in The Crown pub in South London.
He was with Jason. His girlfriend and brother were there, too.

¿Por qué la historia puede no ser verdad? El detective ya tiene otra versión. Escribe una frase: *I think Kevin's story isn't true because…*

Ritmo:

- A: Where were you at seven o'clock yesterday morning?
- B: I was at home.
- A: Were you in bed?
- B: Yes, I was.
- A: Where were you at ten o'clock?
- B: I was in a café with friends.
- A: Were you there all morning?
- B: No, we weren't. We were there for an hour.
