## Qué vas a aprender

- Usar *can / can't* para permiso, posibilidad y habilidad.
- Preguntar y responder: *Can you come at 8.30? No, I can't, but I can come at 10.00.*
- Leer señales: *You can't swim here. You can use the internet here.*
- Concertar una clase o una cita con *can I book…? When can I start?*

## Explicación

*can / can't* (*cannot*) es igual para todas las personas: *I can, she can, they can.* No lleva *to*: *I can park here.* No *I can to park* ni *Do I can sit here?*

Sirve para **permiso** (*Can I park here?*), **posibilidad** (*You can take the test when you are 14*) y **habilidad** (*Can you drive? I can't swim*).

| | + | − |
|---|---|---|
| | I can park here. | I can't park there. |
| ? | Can I park here? | |
| ✓ | Yes, you can. | |
| ✗ | No, you can't. | |

*you* puede ser una persona o la gente en general: *Can you help me?* (tú) frente a *You can't park on a yellow line* (nadie).

Un texto compara el carnet de conducir en el mundo. La palabra que se repite es *test*: *theory test, practical test, driving test.* En Catar puedes examinarte a los 14; en Vietnam el teórico tiene 450 preguntas; en Croacia hacen falta 85 horas de clase; en Brasil, después del examen, un test psicológico.

Anna quiere aprender a conducir. Escribe que necesita un instructor, que el teórico es difícil (*I can't answer the questions. Can you park on a yellow line?*) y que el primer día con su padre es un desastre: *I can't practise in his car.*

Luego llama para reservar clases. Completa con *help, book, start, meet, come*.

En las señales, *can* + verbo dice lo que está permitido; *can't* lo que está prohibido.

Pronunciación: *can* átono es /kən/ (*Where can I park?*). *can* tónico (respuesta corta) es /kæn/ (*Yes, you can.*). *can't* es /kɑːnt/ (UK): *No, you can't.* La diferencia *can / can't* está en esa vocal larga y la *t*.

## Vocabulario

**can y conducir**

- **can / can't** — poder / no poder
- **a driving licence** — un carnet de conducir
- **a driving instructor** — un/a instructor/a
- **a theory test / a practical test** — examen teórico / práctico
- **driving lessons** — clases de conducir
- **pass / fail** — aprobar / suspender
- **park** — aparcar
- **drive** — conducir
- **practise** — practicar
- **book (lessons)** — reservar (clases)

**Señales (verbo + can / can't)**

- **swim** — nadar
- **park** — aparcar
- **have a coffee** — tomar un café
- **pay cash / pay by bank card** — pagar en efectivo / con tarjeta
- **use your phone** — usar el móvil
- **use the internet** — usar internet
- **take photos** — hacer fotos
- **play football** — jugar al fútbol
- **change money** — cambiar dinero
- **drive fast** — ir rápido

**Otras frases**

- **Can I help you?** — ¿En qué puedo ayudarle?
- **I'm free on Monday.** — Estoy libre el lunes.
- **of course** — por supuesto
- **a tourist** — un/a turista

## Frases modelo

- Can you come on Monday at 8.30? No, I can't, but I can come at 10.00.
- I can practise online. I can't answer the questions. Can you come at 8.30? Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
- Where can I park? You can park here.
- Can I park here? Yes, you can. / No, you can't. You can't park here.

Anna y el instructor:

- Instructor: Hello, can I help you?
- Anna: Yes, can I book some driving lessons, please?
- Instructor: Yes, of course.
- Anna: When can I start?
- Instructor: I'm free on Monday. We can meet at your house.
- Anna: Can you come at 8.30?
- Instructor: No, sorry, I can't. I have a lesson at 8.00.
- Anna: OK… Can you come at ten o'clock?
- Instructor: Yes, I can. The lessons are one hour, so 10.00 to 11.00, OK?
- Anna: Great!
- Instructor: What's your name and address?
- Anna: It's Anna Jones…

- You can use the internet here.
- You can't take photos here.

Driving round the world (lee y completa *test*):

- In Qatar, you can take the driving test when you are 14.
- In some states in Mexico, you only need to take a theory test, not a practical test.
- In Vietnam, it's very difficult to pass the theory test. It has 450 questions!
- In Croatia, you need to have 85 hours of driving lessons before you take the practical test.
- In Ukraine, if you do two things wrong in your practical test, you fail.
- In Brazil, after your driving test, you need to pass a psychological test to get your driving licence.
