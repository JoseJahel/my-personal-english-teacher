## Qué vas a aprender

- Dar instrucciones con el imperativo: *Sit down. Don't talk.*
- Usar pronombres de objeto: *me, you, him, her, it, us, them.*
- Nombrar tipos de película: *action, comedy, horror, science fiction…*
- Opinar sobre cine y actores: *I like him. I don't like them. I prefer dramas.*

## Explicación

El imperativo es el infinitivo sin *to*: *Come here. Sit down. Open your books.* La negación es *Don't + infinitivo*: *Don't talk. Don't worry. Don't be late.* Es igual en singular y plural.

Los pronombres de objeto van detrás del verbo o de una preposición. No uses el de sujeto ahí: *Listen to me* (no *Listen to I*). *I love him. Don't talk to her. I want it. They don't speak to us. I want to read them.*

| Sujeto | Objeto |
|---|---|
| I | me |
| you | you |
| he | him |
| she | her |
| it | it |
| we | us |
| they | them |

En un rodaje, el director da órdenes y los actores (Daisy y Sam) hablan de Paul. Lee el diálogo: rodea el verbo bueno y luego di las réplicas en voz alta.

Tipos de cine se relacionan con títulos. *action films* van con *No Time to Die*; *horror* con *The Exorcist*; *animations* con *Frozen*; *comedies* con *Barbie*; etc.

*Do you like Zendaya? Yes, I do. She's very good.* *Do you like horror films? No, I don't like them. I prefer dramas.* El pronombre sustituye el nombre ya dicho: *them* = las películas; *him* = el actor; *her* = la actriz; *it* = la película.

## Vocabulario

**Imperativos útiles**

- **Come in. / Sit down. / Stand up.** — Entra. / Siéntate. / Levántate.
- **Be quiet. / Stay quiet.** — Silencio. / Quédate callado/a.
- **Open / Close the windows.** — Abre / Cierra las ventanas.
- **Listen to me. / Look at him.** — Escúchame. / Míralo.
- **Don't talk. / Don't cry. / Don't move.** — No hables. / No llores. / No te muevas.
- **Don't say anything.** — No digas nada.
- **Cut!** — ¡Corten! (cine)

**Tipos de película**

- **action films** — películas de acción
- **animations** — animación
- **comedies** — comedias
- **dramas** — dramas
- **horror films** — terror
- **science fiction films** — ciencia ficción
- **westerns** — westerns
- **a film / a film series** — una película / una saga

**Opinión**

- **I like him / her / them.** — Me gusta él / ella / ellos.
- **I don't like it.** — No me gusta.
- **I prefer dramas.** — Prefiero los dramas.
- **He's great. / They're awful.** — Es genial. / Son horribles.
- **an actor / an actress** — un actor / una actriz

Títulos para clasificar: *The Exorcist, Scream, The Shining* (horror); *The Magnificent Seven, Django Unchained* (westerns); *Pinocchio, Frozen, Encanto* (animations); *No Time to Die, Mission Impossible, Top Gun: Maverick* (action); *Meet the Parents, Zoolander, Barbie* (comedies); *The Color Purple, The Godfather, Forrest Gump* (dramas); *Aliens, 2001: A Space Odyssey, Avatar* (science fiction).

## Frases modelo

- And Paul? Do you love him? Don't talk about Paul!

En el plató (elige el verbo y luego lee entero):

- Director: OK. Be quiet, please. Scene one, take one. Lights, camera,… action!
- Daisy: Hello, Sam.
- Sam: Hello, Daisy.
- Daisy: Come in. Sit down. We need to talk.
- Sam: Talk? What about?
- Daisy: Us. You and me.
- Sam: Listen to me, Daisy. I love you! Do you love me?
- Daisy: I don't know, Sam. I need time.
- Sam: And Paul? Do you love him?
- Daisy: Don't talk about Paul. Yes, I like Paul, but I don't love him. You don't understand.
- Sam: Don't cry, Daisy. Please don't cry.
- Director: Cut! Great! I like it.
- Director: OK, Scene two. This is a big scene, Daisy, walk to the windows. Open them. Sam, go to Daisy, sit next to her. Daisy, look at him. That's great. Don't move.
- Sam: What do I say to her? I don't remember.
- Director: Nothing. Don't say anything. Just take her hands and look into her eyes. That's it. Cut!

Pronombres:

- 'We need to talk.' 'Talk? What about?' 'Us.'
- 'Listen to me, Daisy.'
- 'I love you! Do you love me?'
- 'I like Paul, but I don't love him.'
- 'Great! I like it.'
- 'Go to the windows. Open them.'

Opiniones:

- A: Do you like Zendaya? B: Yes, I do. She's very good.
- C: Do you like horror films? D: No, I don't like them. I prefer dramas.
- E: Do you like Tom Holland? F: Yes, I like him a lot. I think he's great.

Cinco opiniones (¿a quién o a qué se refiere el pronombre?):

- Speaker 1: It's great. (*the film*)
- Speaker 2: She's OK, but I prefer Zendaya. (*another actress*)
- Speaker 3: I think they're awful. (*the films*)
- Speaker 4: I really like him. I think he's great. (*Tom Holland*)
- Speaker 5: Sorry, I don't know them. (*the actors*)
